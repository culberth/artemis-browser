A prepared XA transaction branch is one whose transaction manager asked this broker to promise to commit, and has not yet said commit or roll back. Until it does, what the branch sent is on no queue, and what it received stays on its queue counted as delivering — with no consumer holding it, and invisible to browse and to the in-flight list.

Only prepared branches are visible here. A branch still active, a JMS transacted session, and the transaction manager’s own log are not reported by the broker. Nothing on this page commits, rolls back or forgets a transaction: only its transaction manager should, and this tool cannot.
