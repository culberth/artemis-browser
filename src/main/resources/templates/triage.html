Triage DLQ back to the queue

Groups a sample of this queue by where each message came from. Artemis records the address and queue a message left when it dead-letters, expires or moves it (_AMQ_ORIG_ADDRESS, _AMQ_ORIG_QUEUE), and the time when it expired one (_AMQ_ACTUAL_EXPIRY). It records no reason: a message killed after its delivery attempts, one an operator sent to the dead-letter address and one moved by hand look the same. So this page says where messages came from and which ones the broker expired — never why the others are here.
